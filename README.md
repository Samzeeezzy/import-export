# Nexora Global Trade — Import & Export Website

A responsive corporate website starter for an import, export and global sourcing business.

## Files
- `index.html` — website structure and content
- `style.css` — responsive visual design
- `script.js` — mobile navigation and enquiry-form preview

## Brand direction
- Deep navy, warm ivory and champagne gold
- Editorial typography and spacious corporate layouts
- Responsive navigation and mobile-friendly sections

## Before launch
1. Confirm the registered business name and company profile.
2. Replace placeholder contact details with verified phone, email and address.
3. Confirm the exact products, services, trade routes and markets served.
4. Connect the enquiry form to a real email/form service.
5. Replace stock photography with approved company and logistics images.
6. Add only verified certifications, registrations and operational claims.

The enquiry form is currently a front-end preview and does not send or store submissions.
